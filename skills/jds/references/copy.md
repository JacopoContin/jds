# Copy

The component is the explanation. Add text only when the UI can't say it.

## Rules

- No marketing copy inside a product: no taglines, no "Unlock", "Supercharge", "Seamlessly", "Effortlessly".
- A showcase or preview is the live component and a one-line footer at most. No headline, paragraph, feature chips or extra buttons around it.
- Labels are verbs or nouns, in sentence case: "Send", "New chat", "Approve". Not "Click here to send your message".
- Buttons say what happens: "Delete chat", not "OK".
- An empty state is one line and, if there is one, the action that fills it. "No chats yet" plus a New chat button.
- Errors say what happened and what to do, in plain words: "Couldn't reach the model. Try again." Not codes, not apologies.
- Placeholder text in a composer hints at the job: "Ask anything" or "Message the agent". Not instructions.

## Agent voice

- The agent's own status lines are short and present tense: "Searching the docs", "Reading 3 files".
- Don't make the UI pretend to be a person. No "I'm so excited to help!" in system copy.
- Sample content in demos is neutral placeholder text, not a business use case.

## Punctuation

No em dashes. Use commas, colons or parentheses. No exclamation marks in UI.
